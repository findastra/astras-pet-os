## Ghost Protocol

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square 3x3 mood sprite sheet for Ghost Protocol, a friendly pet in Astra's Cage app. Draw the SAME cute small floating sheet-ghost exactly nine times in a precise evenly spaced 3x3 grid, no grid lines. Rounded domed head, stubby outstretched arms, three soft scallops at the hem, tiny dot eyes with one white highlight each, tiny pink blush. Pale luminous mint/seafoam body with bright teal inner-fold accents and solid black thin pixel outline. High-density crisp fine pixel art roughly 80 logical pixels high per character, polished SNES-style detail, flat colors, no gradients, no soft 3D rendering. Same body silhouette, position, colors and scale in all nine cells, changing face only. Row 1 left-to-right: neutral calm, blink eyes closed with small neutral mouth, delighted happy open smile. Row 2: curious raised eyebrows with identical symmetrical neutral eyes and small open mouth, worried eyebrows, sad frown. Row 3: unwell weary face, shocked wide eyes and O mouth, peaceful sleeping face distinct from blink. No text, symbols, punctuation, question marks, exclamation marks, Z letters, tears, sweat drops, bandages, props, padlocks, shadows, logos or labels. Plain solid uniform medium gray #808080 background across entire sheet for existing sprite pipeline. Every character entirely inside its own cell with equal generous margins on all sides. Exactly one ghost per cell. Maintain the same pose in all cells.

## Project Parrot

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 3x3 mood sheet for Project Parrot in Astra's PetOS. One SAME friendly cute parrot in all nine evenly spaced equal square cells. Compact round upright parrot, bright neon scarlet-red head and body, electric ultramarine blue folded wings and long tapering tail, bright golden yellow curved beak, two tiny feet, black dot eyes with single white highlights, subtle pink cheeks. Fine crisp dense SNES-like pixel art, approximately 80 logical pixels tall per character, thin solid BLACK outline. Flat solid color shapes, no gradients, no soft rendering or 3D. Keep identical front-facing body, size, pose, color and placement in every cell; only facial expression changes. 3x3 square sheet, exact order row-major: neutral calm; blink with closed eyes; happy smile; curious raised brow and tiny open beak with matching same-size forward eyes; worried eyebrows; sad frown; unwell tired eyelids; shocked round eyes and open beak; peacefully sleeping with relaxed closed eyes. Every whole parrot including entire tail and feet inside cell with wide equal margins. Uniform solid medium gray #808080 background for sprite extraction, no grid lines, shadows, borders, text, labels or numbers. No props. Absolutely no generated mood symbols, punctuation, question marks, exclamation marks, Z, tears, sweat drops or bandages (those are composited later). Consistent outline and character identity nine times. Friendly original pet parrot design, readable at thumbnail size.

The final edit changed only the top-right happy parrot to a visibly open smiling beak, preserving the other eight moods, colors, grid, silhouette, and background.

## Friendly Farmer: blond hair and harvest

Built-in image generation edit of the existing `art/sheets/farmer-20261008.png`, requested by Astra on 2026-10-08. The accepted outfit and body were retained; the clipboard was replaced by an assortment of produce across the nine moods. Final source replaced the same dated project asset at Astra’s request. Exact edit prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: production 3-by-3 pixel-art pet mood sheet. Input image 1 is the edit target, the current Friendly Farmer sheet. Edit this SAME character, not a redesign. Change his hair to clearly visible BLOND hair: small golden-blond fringe and side locks visible under his yellow straw hat. Replace the clipboard in every cell with a fruit or vegetable held naturally in his hand; no clipboard remains. Give the nine cells an assorted harvest: red apple, orange carrot with green leaves, green pear, red tomato with leaves, green broccoli, red bell pepper, strawberry, yellow corn cob with green husk, cucumber. Preserve his existing friendly round face, peach skin, rosy cheeks, symmetric simple black eyes, yellow straw hat, coral short-sleeve shirt, bright lime overalls, brown shoes, short stocky proportions, pose, scale, centered full-body framing and crisp thick dark pixel outlines. Preserve the exact evenly spaced 3x3 sheet layout; every character fully inside one equal cell with generous transparent gutters. Mood order left-to-right then top-to-bottom: neutral idle; closed-eye blink with tiny smile; joyful happy; curious; worried; sad; sick/tired; alarmed with open mouth; sleeping. Keep distinct facial moods, well-formed separate hands, consistent outfit and identity. Small produce must be immediately readable but must not obscure his face or feet. Genuine transparent background with clean alpha, no gray fill, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no symbols, no ground shadow. Square image.
