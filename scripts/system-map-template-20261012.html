Moving around

Left-drag or scroll-click drag turns the map freely; Shift + scroll-click drag pans. Scroll zooms toward the centre, or toward what you selected. Right-drag looks around; while holding it, W A S D fly, Q/E down and up, Shift faster. F frames what you selected. Esc closes the card. On a phone: one finger turns, two fingers zoom and pan.
