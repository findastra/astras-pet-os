### Its tools (code it runs with no AI)

- **pi-display-guide-tool**: Build guide for a Raspberry Pi 4 on the back of the official 7" Touch Display: fins, ribbon, wires, fan and lid, SD card, power on and what to do if the screen stays black. Ticks stay in this browser.
- **pi-pin-map-tool**: All 40 GPIO pins of a Raspberry Pi 4, each with its own key line, and the four the touch-display build uses: display power on 4 and 6, fan on 2 and 14.
