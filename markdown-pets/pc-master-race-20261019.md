### Its tools (code it runs with no AI)

- **learn-computer-organization-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): six steps from the transistor up (Code; Nand2Tetris; Ben Eater; Patterson and Hennessy; Computer Systems: A Programmer’s Perspective; Windows Internals), simulators to try, and the memory ladder filled in with her own CPU, caches, memory and drives. Astra: "I want to know more about computer organization." Ticks stay in this browser.
- **pc-look-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): pc-master-race-20261103.mjs --watch: one look every ten minutes (winget and WizTree hourly), reading only. It runs each tool’s quiet export, hidden, never as administrator, and never accepts a license for Astra. What it finds stays on the PC (Documents\Astra’s PetOS Data\pc-master-race). Not started at sign-in yet.
- **everything-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): Everything’s own web search, which answers on this PC only, inside this tool box while Everything runs; the age of its index; the twelve biggest files through es.exe. File Master keeps using Everything for finding files (a tool can sit on several pets, Astra, 2026-11-03).
- **spacesniffer-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): each drive’s size, free space and how full it is, from Windows. SpaceSniffer has no quiet export, so its map of boxes stays in its own window. File Master keeps it too.
- **wiztree-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): WizTree’s own CSV export of her user folder, one level deep and without administrator (/admin=0), every hour.
- **usbdeview-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): USBDeview’s own hidden CSV export, plugged-in devices first. Rift Rabbit’s dongle map runs the same program.
- **cpu-z-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): Windows’ own numbers for the parts CPU-Z reports (CPU-Z asks for administrator each time, so its full report stays in its window), with a note when memory runs below the speed its part number names.
- **hwinfo-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): whether HWiNFO64 is on the PC, its version and whether it runs. Its sensors stay in its own window; feeding them here from its sensor log is not built.
- **crystaldiskinfo-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): each drive’s health, kind and connection from Windows’ own check; CrystalDiskInfo reads every SMART number in its own window, with administrator.
- **latencymon-tool** (planned, not built yet): A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): held, not installed: why is on its box and on LatencyMon’s card.
- **process-explorer-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): the twelve programs using the most memory, from Windows. Process Explorer’s tree, handles and VirusTotal check stay in its window; stopping programs stays Kernel Koala’s job.
- **autoruns-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): autorunsc’s CSV (sign-in and scheduled tasks, Microsoft’s own hidden), once Astra has accepted the Sysinternals license herself. Switching items off stays with Kernel Grove’s startup switch.
- **process-monitor-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): whether Process Monitor is on the PC and its version; recording needs administrator, so it runs only when Astra starts it.
- **rammap-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): memory in use and the biggest users, from Windows; RAMMap’s page-by-page map needs administrator and stays in its window.
- **tcpview-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): tcpvcon’s CSV counted per program (talking, listening), once Astra has accepted the Sysinternals license herself. Packet Piranha owns how well the network works.
- **coreinfo-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): Coreinfo’s core and cache map, once Astra has accepted the Sysinternals license herself.
- **winobj-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): whether WinObj is on the PC; it browses Windows’ object namespace in its own window, a companion to Windows Internals.
- **handle-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): whether Handle is on the PC. It needs administrator, so an assistant runs it only with Astra’s OK; PowerToys’ File Locksmith does the same without.
- **sigcheck-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): every tool’s signature as Windows checks it, on every look; Sigcheck goes deeper when asked.
- **powertoys-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): which PowerToys tools are switched on and off, from PowerToys’ own settings.
- **winget-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): winget’s list of updates waiting (winget source only), hourly. Nothing is updated from here: Patch Cat keeps programs patched, and an update waits for Astra’s OK.
- **wireshark-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): the adapters dumpcap -D lists. Recording happens only in Wireshark’s own window; Packet Piranha owns network checks.
- **windows-performance-recorder-tool**: A tool of PC Master Race (Astra, 2026-11-03): whether wpr.exe is there; recording needs administrator, so it runs only when Astra asks.
