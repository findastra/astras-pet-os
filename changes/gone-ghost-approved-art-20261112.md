## Why the draft was showing

Since 2026-10-20 a pet's page uses the newest drawing of each mood in `desktop-art/<art>/` first, so the page and the
desktop pet are one pet (`petFrames` in `scripts/pet-program-pages-20261018.mjs`). Gone Ghost's only drawings there were
the 2026-10-11 draft, so his page, his mood pictures (`art/moods/ghost-protocol/`, `build-mood-stickers-20261024.mjs`),
`art/latest/desktop-art/ghost-protocol/` and his icon (`make-icons-20261012.mjs` read `idle-20261011.png` by name)
showed the draft.
