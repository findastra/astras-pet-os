# Light Hive's rainforest storm plays real recordings (2026-11-13)

Astra, 2026-11-13, on the deck "Rainforest storm: where the sound comes from": "all recommended" (free CC0 real
recordings, mixed per preset, thunder still timed to the lightning; keep all twelve presets). Then: "Yes, download them".

- `apps/sounds/`: fourteen CC0 recordings from freesound.org (rain, drops on big leaves, drips, a tin roof, a
  downpour, a waterfall pool, birds, night insects, frogs, wind, two distant thunders and a close strike), cut short,
  re-encoded small with even loudness, about 10 MB. Makers and links in `apps/sounds/storm-sounds-credits-20261113.md`
  and in the tool's More details.
- `apps/rgbee-rainforest-storm-20261108.html`: each preset has a `mix` of the recordings. Each recording plays as
  overlapping 12-26 s pieces from random places, slightly faster or slower, crossfaded, so it never repeats; with
  Keeps changing on, every recording drifts on its own. Thunder is real, cut to start where it hits, lower and later
  the farther the strike; a close strike rolls on with a distant one. A limiter keeps close strikes from distorting.
  The lightning clock, the three-flashes-a-second limit and the lights are unchanged. If the recordings can't load
  (a copy opened straight from a file), the sound made in the page plays instead.
- Dawn after rain and Sleep were nearly silent with the recordings, so their rain, life and volume went up.

Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`), 2026-11-13.
