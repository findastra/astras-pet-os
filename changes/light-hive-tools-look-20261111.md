# Light Hive's two tools, easier to read, with presets (2026-11-11)

Astra, 2026-11-11: "I don't like the extra black panel behind the sync my lights, and it's kind of like hard to read...
make it more like aesthetically pleasing and user friendly. The rainforest one too, neither of them have the more
details in the tool... I like being able to change the rainstorm, but that one's going to need a lot of presets... it
needs to be more variable."

- Both tools, inside their tool boxes on Light Hive's page, have no background of their own and tell the box how tall
  they are (the house way, like PC Master Race's tools), so no black block sits behind them. Larger, lighter text.
- Each tool has its own **More details** with the four lines every panel uses (how it works, where it comes from,
  your rules, not built yet).
- `apps/rgbee-sync-my-lights-20261108.html`: a big colour circle (click it for any colour), the scenes as colour
  chips, brightness with its percentage, and the lights grouped by what reaches them now, what they wait for, and the
  ones with no lights. The Corsair keyboard and MSI motherboard now say they need their own apps: OpenRGB 1.1 cannot
  set either (checked on Astra's PC, 2026-11-11).
- `apps/rgbee-rainforest-storm-20261108.html`: the picture on top with Play in it, the controls under it instead of
  over it. Twelve presets (Light drizzle, Steady rain, Rain on big leaves, Under a tin roof, Distant storm, Storm
  overhead, Monsoon downpour, Night jungle, Dawn after rain, Waterfall pool, Sleep, Surprise me), each with its own
  sound and its own colour for the lights between strikes. New sounds: single drops, frogs, a waterfall, close strikes
  that crack before they rumble. **Keeps changing by itself** lets the rain, wind and life drift like weather. The
  three-flashes-a-second limit and the clock-shared storm are unchanged.
- Real recorded sound instead of sound made in the page waits on Astra's answer (deck, 2026-11-11).
