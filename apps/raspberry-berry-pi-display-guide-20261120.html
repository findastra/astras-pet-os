Pi 4 + 7″ Touch Display

Raspberry Pi Maker's build guide: the Pi mounted on the back of the official 7″ Touch Display, with fins and a fan.

before you start

Unplug all power. Leave the orange ribbon and the thin touch ribbon on the display board alone. Red always goes to 5V and black always to GND. Swapping them can damage the boards.

0 of 0 done

1 · fins (heatsinks)

  1. 1Peel each pad and press for 10 s, fins up: big on the silver CPU, medium on the RAM beside it, small on the USB chip and the network chip.
  2. 2Keep them off each other, off the 4 little pins by the Ethernet port, and away from the ribbon.

2 · flat ribbon

  1. 3Display board (RPI-DISPLAY): slide the tan bar out 1-2 mm. Insert silver stripes up, blue tab down, then slide the bar back.
  2. 4Pi (DISPLAY): lift the black top 1-2 mm. Bring the ribbon over in a smooth U with no twist, push it straight in, then press the top down. Don't flip that end.
  3. 5Tug each end gently. Neither should move.

3 · wires

  1. 6Display board (5V, INT, SDA, SCL, GND): red on 5V, black on GND. Leave the middle three empty. Don't use the yellow or green wires.
  2. 7Screw the Pi onto the 4 brass posts. The ribbon loop and wires go out the side, not under the board.
  3. 8Pi: display red on pin 4, display black on pin 6. The outer row is only even pins, so these sit side by side with no gaps (see the pin map tool).

4 · fan + lid

  1. 9Fan red on pin 2, fan black on pin 14. The fan sits in the clear lid, over the CPU heatsink.
  2. 10The lid has nothing to clip to. Hold it with 2-3 squares of double-sided foam tape on the metal tops of the USB/Ethernet ports. Never put tape on chips or pins.
  3. 11Before pressing it down: the ribbon isn't pinched, the fan spins freely, and the spare wire is folded away from the blades.

5 · sd card + power on

  1. 12Put Raspberry Pi OS (or RetroPie) on a microSD card with Raspberry Pi Imager. This erases the card.
  2. 13Slide the card into the slot on the underside of the Pi, at the end opposite the USB ports, gold contacts facing the Pi.
  3. 14Plug power into the Pi's USB-C only, never the display board's micro-USB. The green light should flicker.

if something's wrong

You seeDo this
Green light solid, no flickerNo working SD card. Check that there is one and it has Raspberry Pi OS on it.
Black screen, green flickersRecheck both ribbon ends and both latches. The screen stays fully dark until the Pi starts talking to it.
No backlightRecheck the red/black wires: 5V/GND on the display, pins 4 and 6 on the Pi.
Picture, no touchReseat the thin touch ribbon on the display board.
Fan doesn't spinRecheck pins 2 and 14.
