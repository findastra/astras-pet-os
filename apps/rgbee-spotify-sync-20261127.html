    More details
    How it works
    Press Start the show and play something on Spotify on this PC. Pick a preset or change any setting: it goes to the lights straight away and is saved on the PC. The live view shows what the server hears: five parts of the sound from bass to treble, the line from the lowest to the highest notes, and the dot changes colour on each beat.
    Where it comes from
    Light Hive’s server on this PC (findastra/rgbee server/, light-hive-server 1.5.0 and later). It hears Windows’ loopback of the default speakers (Voicemeeter Input on this PC, where Spotify plays), splits the sound into bands and finds the beats. Nothing is recorded or saved. Only your PC’s copy of this page reaches it; the live site only previews.
    Your rules
    2026-11-27: every beat changes the colour, parts of the line answer different parts of the sound, the line is the wave length, lots of settings to experiment with, solid blocks. Never more than three changes a second (the shortest gap is 350 ms); nothing on this page moves when your computer asks for less motion.
    Not built yet
    The server doesn’t start by itself at sign-in, and the show doesn’t come back on after a restart (your settings do). Music playing on another device can’t be heard: pick this PC in Spotify Connect. MSI’s lights aren’t reached yet.
