How it works
Pick a scene or click the big circle for any colour, set the brightness, and press Send. Every light Light Hive reaches takes it at once; the others say what they are waiting for.
Where it comes from
Light Hive’s helper on this PC (findastra/rgbee, helper/): Razer through the Razer Chroma App, Govee over your Wi-Fi once LAN Control is on. Only a page opened from your PC’s copy reaches it; the live site only shows what the lights would do.
