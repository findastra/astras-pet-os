How it works
Pick a preset, then press Play. The rain, wind and forest are made live as it plays, so it never loops; with Keeps changing on, they drift by themselves like real weather. Lightning flashes the picture and every light Light Hive reaches, and the thunder follows: the closer the strike, the sooner and louder. Between strikes your lights hold the preset’s colour. Full screen turns the screen itself into a light.
Where it comes from
The sound is made in this page by your browser, so there are no recordings and nothing to download. The lights go through Light Hive’s helper on this PC (findastra/rgbee), only from your PC’s copy of the page. Two screens on the same lightning level strike at the same seconds, so the desktop and the laptop flash together.
