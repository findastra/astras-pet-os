why-the-zeros

Each character of a fingerprint is one of 16 (0–9, a–f), so the chance a guess starts with one 0 is 1 in 16. Two zeros: 1 in 16 × 16 = 256. Each zero character is 4 zero bits, which is how lessons 3 and 5 count difficulty.

Variables

  • z = zero characters needed at the start
  • G = guesses needed on average

G = 16z

  1. z = 3: G = 16 × 16 × 16 = 4,096 guesses
  2. z = 6: G = 16,777,216 guesses, a few seconds for this page at full speed

Bitcoin needs about 20 zero characters right now: the whole network's 1,000 EH/s × 600 seconds a block is roughly 600,000 billion billion guesses for every block (late 2026, approx.).
