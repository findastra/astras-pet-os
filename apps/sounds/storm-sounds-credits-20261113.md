# Rainforest storm recordings (Light Hive's rainforest-storm-tool)

Every file here is a real recording from [freesound.org](https://freesound.org), released by its maker under
**Creative Commons 0** (CC0: anyone may use it, no permission or payment needed). Each licence was checked on the
sound's own page on 2026-11-13. Thank you to the makers.

The files are Freesound's preview copies, cut short and re-encoded small (stereo, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3) with even
loudness (beds about -24 LUFS, thunder about -18 LUFS). The frogs were recorded on the right side only, so that side
plays on both.
